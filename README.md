# ANTARA

### A survivor-controlled safety and case-readiness system for domestic and coercive abuse.

> **What does personal safety look like when the danger lives with you?**

ANTARA is designed for survivors who may still be living with an abuser — where an obvious safety application, repeatedly explaining what happened, or keeping sensitive information on a single device can itself create risk.

ANTARA provides a discreet, survivor-controlled journey from documenting an incident to preparing information for help-seeking.

## The Journey

**RECORD → STRUCTURE → PRESERVE → PREPARE → NAVIGATE → HANDOFF**

### 01 — Record
Capture what happened in the survivor's own words without requiring them to identify legal or abuse categories themselves.

### 02 — Structure
The Case Readiness Engine organises incidents, identifies relevant patterns and evidence, and builds an evolving Case Record.

### 03 — Preserve
Sensitive evidence and essential documents can be preserved through the Guardian Vault, reducing dependence on a single device.

### 04 — Prepare
ANTARA structures available information into preparation packs for:

- Domestic violence
- Economic abuse
- Cyber abuse

### 05 — Navigate
Provides explainable recommendations for relevant verified support services based on the survivor's recorded circumstances.

### 06 — Handoff
The survivor chooses what categories and evidence to include before generating a structured handoff bundle.

**Nothing is automatically sent. The survivor remains in control.**

---

## Discreet Access

ANTARA initially appears as a casual game.

A private gesture reveals the protected access layer, while **Quick Exit** allows the user to immediately return to the disguised interface.

This design recognises that, in coercive environments, simply having an identifiable safety application may increase risk.

---

## Core Principle

> **Tell your story once. Preserve it beyond one device. Carry it wherever you choose to seek help.**

ANTARA is not designed to diagnose abuse, replace legal professionals, automatically file complaints, or contact authorities without consent.

It is a **preparation, preservation, navigation and consent-controlled handoff layer** between experiencing abuse and seeking help.

## Prototype Scope

ANTARA is currently a hackathon prototype.

The current implementation demonstrates the end-to-end survivor journey and system architecture. Production deployment would require additional security hardening, persistent infrastructure, formal verification of service information, and appropriate legal/privacy review.
